OpaGachi
========

Little animated desk pets that sit above your taskbar and act out what your AI agents are doing:
thinking, working, waiting for your approval, failed, or done.

Works with the Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok desktop apps, and with Claude Code, Codex,
Gemini CLI and Grok Build.

Getting started
---------------
1. Run "OpaGachi Setup.exe". It installs for your Windows account only (no admin rights needed),
   by default into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OpaGachi, and adds OpaGachi to the Start menu.
2. OpaGachi starts. A welcome window shows which AI apps it found.
3. Open any AI app and its pet appears. Double-click the red ring in the system tray for Settings.

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else to install.

Updating
--------
Run the newer "OpaGachi Setup.exe". It finds your install, closes OpaGachi, replaces the program
files and keeps your settings, custom sprites, uploaded ties and sounds.

Your data
---------
OpaGachi runs entirely on your PC and never sends anything over the internet. Its settings, its log
and any sprites, ties or sounds you add are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpaGachi, apart from the program
files. Settings > Privacy and legal explains exactly what it reads and why.

To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > OpaGachi > Uninstall. The uninstaller takes
OpaGachi's hooks out of your agents' settings first, and asks whether to delete your data too.
