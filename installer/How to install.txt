OpaGachi: how to install
========================

1. Unzip this file: right-click it > Extract All > Extract.
2. Double-click "OpaGachi Setup.exe" in the unzipped folder.
3. Keep the suggested folder (or choose another), then click Install.
   No admin rights are needed; it installs for your Windows account only.
4. OpaGachi starts. Look for the red ring in the system tray (bottom right, next to the clock);
   double-click it for Settings.

Updating: run a newer "OpaGachi Setup.exe" the same way. Your settings and custom sprites are kept.
Uninstalling: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > OpaGachi > Uninstall.

If Windows stops the installer
------------------------------
- "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen): click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- "Smart App Control blocked an app": this copy isn't signed by a verified publisher, and
  Smart App Control has no override. Use a PC where Smart App Control is off, or a signed release.

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else to install.
